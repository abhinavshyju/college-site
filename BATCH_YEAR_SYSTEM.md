# Batch Year System

## Overview
The college has transitioned from 3-year to 4-year UG degree programs starting from the 2024 batch.

## Batch Year Ranges

### 3-Year UG Programs (Old System)
- **2021 Batch**: 2021-2024 (Graduated)
-**2022 Batch**: 2022-2025 (Final year in 2025)
- **2023 Batch**: 2023-2026 (Final year in 2026)

### 4-Year UG Programs (New System - NEP 2020)
- **2024 Batch**: 2024-2028 (4 years)
- **2025 Batch**: 2025-2029 (4 years)
- **Future batches**: Continue with 4-year format

### PG Programs
- All PG programs remain **2-year** duration

## Usage in System

### Admin Panel Input
When adding attendance or internal marks records, enter the batch year in the format:
- Old batches: `2021-2024`, `2022-2025`, `2023-2026`
- New batches: `2024-2028`, `2025-2029`

### Public Filter
The Academics section shows a batch filter dropdown with these predefined options matching the above formats.

## Why This Matters
This ensures accurate filtering and display of academic records, allowing students and faculty to find records specific to their batch year and program duration.
